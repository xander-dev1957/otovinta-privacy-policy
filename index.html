OtoVinta Privacy Policy

OtoVinta — Independent App Developer

Last updated: October 17, 2026

OtoVinta is a mobile application for automotive sales professionals. This policy explains how information is handled through the app, including local storage, user-directed sharing, and third-party services. OtoVinta is initially intended for distribution in the Philippines and may become available elsewhere.

1. Information handled by the app

Depending on use, the app can store customer names, contact details, addresses, notes, photographs, documents, vehicle interests, inquiries, test drives, quotations, reservations, financing information, follow-ups, sales records, and reports. It can also store salesperson and business profile details, app settings, and security preferences. Uploaded documents may contain additional personal information supplied by the user.

2. Local storage and accounts

OtoVinta is designed as a local-first application. Customer and business records are stored in the app's local storage on the user's device. OtoVinta currently does not require an online account or provide developer-operated cloud synchronization of these records. Android automatic cloud backup is disabled for the app.

3. Sharing and exporting

Users may choose to export or share quotations, reports, customer documents, and other files using Android's sharing, saving, printing, or email features. These files may contain personal information. Once shared, information is handled by the selected destination or receiving application under its own practices.

Users can also create password-encrypted backup files and perform encrypted local device-to-device transfers. Previously created legacy backup files may use older protection formats. The separate vehicle-catalog data-sharing feature does not intentionally include customer records. Users are responsible for protecting exported files, backup passwords, and recipient choices.

4. Advertising and third-party services

OtoVinta uses Google AdMob for advertising in eligible app versions. Google's advertising services may process advertising identifiers, IP addresses, device and app information, and ad interaction information, subject to Google's policies. The app may initialize advertising services even when an ad is not displayed. OtoVinta also uses Google-provided document and code scanning services, which may process technical or diagnostic information according to their terms.

Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Security

OtoVinta uses Android app-private storage and provides optional PIN or biometric app locking. New-format backups use password-based encryption, and supported local transfers use authenticated encryption. The app also uses controls intended to limit accidental screen and notification exposure. Local application database storage is not separately encrypted at the database level. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection.

6. Retention and deletion

Locally stored records generally remain until the user removes them or the app's data is erased. OtoVinta provides customer anonymization features intended to remove identifying details and related files. Certain business records may remain identifiable when a documented retention hold applies; the user or business must determine whether such retention is legally appropriate. Copies already exported, shared, or backed up may need to be handled separately. Uninstalling the app ordinarily removes its local app-private data, but not files saved elsewhere.

7. Responsibilities and privacy requests

The salesperson or business using OtoVinta generally determines why and how customer information is collected and used, and is responsible for applicable privacy notices, lawful handling, retention decisions, and responses to customer requests. Customers seeking access, correction, or deletion of records entered by a salesperson should normally contact that salesperson or business. Requests about data processed by the app developer or its integrated services may be sent to the contact below.

8. Applicable laws and rights

Depending on applicable law and circumstances, individuals may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to certain processing of personal information, and other statutory rights. OtoVinta's initial Philippine distribution may be subject to Republic Act No. 10173 (the Data Privacy Act of 2012) and related rules. Other laws may apply if the app is offered or used in additional jurisdictions.

9. Children's privacy

OtoVinta is intended for professional and business use and is not directed to children.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated when app features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this page with a revised last-updated date.
